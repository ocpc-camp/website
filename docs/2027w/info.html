Camp information

Contest format

Each edition includes seven five-hour contests for teams of up to three people. After each contest, an author or coach discusses the solutions. Choose one site because Hong Kong and Osijek use the same contests.

Rules

The contests generally follow standard ICPC rules.

  • Teams rank first by the number of problems solved, then by the lowest total time. For each solved problem, we count the time until acceptance plus 20 minutes for each rejected submission on that problem. If teams are still tied, the earlier last acceptance wins.
  • If a problem statement is unclear or contains an error, ask the judges for clarification. They usually answer “Yes”, “No” or “No comments”.
  • The public scoreboard updates during the contest and freezes for the final hour.

Use one computer at a time per team. Onsite teams share a machine. If you compete online from different places, take turns using your computers. You may read statements on a second screen. A program started on the active computer may keep running after you switch, provided nobody interacts with that computer until your team switches back.

You may use the internet during a contest. For ICPC practice, we strongly recommend using an ICPC-compliant Team Reference Document instead. During the contest, do not communicate with anyone outside your team or with a large language model. This includes discussing problems, posting questions on forums and prompting ChatGPT.

Terms and conditions of the contest system apply.

Contest environment

We run the contests on Eolymp. You can read how submissions are tested. Participants will receive credentials by email.

Camp materials and silence period

We will have a silence period that lasts for at least 6 months after the camp, during which camp materials will not be released to the public. Therefore, we ask participants not to discuss the problems in public during that period.

After the camp ends, we will give all participants access to camp materials for private use. Please do not publish them without consulting the authors or camp organizers.

Most of our contests are fresh and developed for this camp. A small number of contests may be based on previous contests that have not been released to the general public. If you have seen some problems of a contest before, you can't participate on that day (and your participation fee will be reduced accordingly). We will privately contact participants who might be affected.

Onsite rules

We encourage onsite teams to bring their own device. Computers provided by the host university may be available on request, but their number is limited.

Onsite teams will have access to a printer.

Visa & invitation letters

If you need a visa to attend, please register as early as possible and let us know. The host university can provide invitation letters for participants who require them.
