Visiting Hong Kong

Location

The camp runs on 2–8 January 2027 at City University of Hong Kong, Tat Chee Avenue, Kowloon Tong. Room and campus access instructions will follow.

Getting there

Take the MTR to Kowloon Tong station, served by the East Rail and Kwun Tong lines. Walk through Festival Walk to the CityU campus. See the campus directory and maps.

From Hong Kong International Airport, use the Airport Express and onward MTR connections, or check the airport’s bus and taxi options when planning your journey.

Accommodation

Teams arrange and pay for their accommodation. CityUHK Lodge is an on-campus option; check availability and current rates directly. For other accommodation, look for convenient MTR access to Kowloon Tong.

Food

Regular meals are at your own expense. There are dining outlets on campus and restaurants in Festival Walk next to the university. Check opening hours, particularly at weekends.

Let us know about dietary or accessibility needs in the registration form.

Registration and visa invitations

Register your team and check the deadlines. If you need a visa invitation letter, request it in the form early. For venue or access questions, see Contact.
