Hong Kong schedule

Seven contests on seven consecutive days, 2–8 January 2027.

All times are in HKT (UTC+8). Times and activities may change; follow Discord for updates.

Online teams can start each contest within 24 hours of its onsite start and receive the full five hours. Solution discussions follow the onsite timetable; recordings will be available afterwards.
