What is OCPC?

OCPC is a training camp for university teams preparing for ICPC and similar competitions. Both winter editions use the same seven contests, so choose one edition.

Online teams can start each contest within 24 hours of its onsite start and get the full five-hour run. Solution discussions follow the onsite schedule, with recordings available afterwards.

See the Hong Kong schedule or Osijek schedule. Please keep the problems private until their release is authorized.

Participation fees

Fees are per team of up to three people:

  • €198 regular.
  • €147 early bird: register by 2 December 2026 for Hong Kong or 30 December 2026 for Osijek.
  • €48 hardship, based on your own assessment of financial need.
  • Free for Hong Kong onsite participants, people affected by the war in Ukraine and teams affiliated with Ukrainian institutions.

Registration

Teams cover their own travel, accommodation and regular meals. Onsite places are subject to confirmation. If you need a visa invitation letter, register early.
